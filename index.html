Butter Chicken Bunny Chow

Transcribed from reel

Chicken & Marinade

  • 4 chicken fillets, cubed
  • 4 thigh fillets, cubed
  • 6 tbsp plain yoghurt
  • 3 tsp ginger garlic
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • 1½ tsp crushed cumin
  • 1½ tsp crushed coriander seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Sauce

  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1½ tsp ginger garlic
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 50 g tomato paste
  • 40 g butter
  • Cream, to finish

Soft White Loaf (bunny chow bread)

  • 4 cups cake flour
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 2½ tsp sugar
  • 1¼ tsp salt
  • ⅓ cup oil
  • ¾ cup lukewarm milk
  • ¾ cup lukewarm water

Steps

  1. Add the chicken to a bowl with the yoghurt, ginger garlic, lemon juice, cumin, crushed coriander seeds, turmeric, chilli powder, salt and pepper. Mix well, cover and marinate for a few hours.
  2. Heat a heavy pot over medium-high heat and add the marinated chicken. Cook until nicely browned and almost cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, add the ghee and onion; cook until softened and lightly golden.
  4. Add the ginger garlic, coriander powder, garam masala, turmeric and chilli powder; cook briefly until fragrant.
  5. Add the tomato paste and butter; cook down a few minutes until the tomato paste darkens slightly and the butter begins to separate.
  6. Return the chicken to the pan and mix well so it's coated in sauce. Simmer until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce thickens.
  7. Finish with a generous splash of cream, stir through, taste and adjust seasoning. Top with coriander and serve.

Mix, knead and proof the dough, bake into a loaf, hollow out and fill with the butter chicken.

Chicken Sukka

Transcribed from reel

A spicy, aromatic South Indian dish with freshly ground spices and golden fried coconut. Serve with rice, roti or dosa.

Steps

  1. To a pan, add a little oil plus cloves, cardamom, cinnamon, bay leaf, coriander seeds, cumin seeds and dry red chillies. Sauté until fragrant. Add a small tamarind piece at the end and blend everything into a smooth paste.
  2. In the same pan, fry freshly grated coconut until golden brown. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add a little more oil, then sliced onions. Sauté until softened and lightly golden.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté until fragrant.
  5. Add the chicken, salt and turmeric. Mix well and sauté until the chicken is about 80% cooked.
  6. Add the ground masala paste and mix well. Cook until the masala is nicely cooked and starts releasing oil.
  7. Add the fried coconut and sauté everything well until the chicken is fully cooked and the flavours come together.
  8. Finish with a handful of fresh coriander leaves, give one last mix, and serve hot.

Chicken & Chickpea Kebabs

Transcribed from reel

Protein-packed and healthy if air-fried.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups soaked chickpeas (1 cup raw chickpeas soaked overnight)
  • 1 chicken breast
  • 1 potato
  • 2 onions
  • 1 tbsp coriander seeds
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 10–15 black peppercorns
  • 4 dried red chillies
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala powder
  • Salt
  • ½ cup water

Steps

  1. In a pressure cooker add the soaked chickpeas, chicken breast, potato, onions, the whole spices, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, garam masala, salt and ½ cup water. Cook until done.
  2. Open the lid and remove the chicken breast; shred it.
  3. For the chickpea mixture, switch on the flame and dry the mixture completely, mashing with a masher for a smooth texture. Add 1–2 tbsp ghee.
  4. Add the shredded chicken and mix well.
  5. Transfer to a bowl and let cool. Then add 1 egg, coriander leaves, 2–3 green chillies and 1 tbsp lemon juice.
  6. Mix well, shape into kebabs and fry in hot oil (or air-fry for a healthier version).

Broccoli Cheddar Soup

Transcribed from reel

Panera-style, perfect for soup season.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 shallot, diced
  • 3 tbsp minced garlic
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp nutmeg
  • ½ tsp ground mustard
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup milk (added ½ at a time)
  • 1 tbsp chicken bouillon
  • 2–3 cups extra sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • Cheddar Bay biscuits (see box directions)
  • Garnish: diced bacon and extra cheddar

Steps

  1. Sauté the onion, shallot, garlic and carrots in the butter until soft.
  2. Stir in the flour and spices (smoked paprika, nutmeg, ground mustard) to make a roux.
  3. Whisk in the chicken stock, then the cream and milk.
  4. Add the broccoli and bouillon; simmer until the broccoli is tender.
  5. Stir in the shredded cheddar until melted and creamy.
  6. Serve with a warm Cheddar Bay biscuit, garnished with bacon and cheddar.

Everyday Chai (makes 2 cups)

Transcribed from reel

A deeply spiced everyday chai for two.

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup water
  • 1¾ cups milk
  • 5 cardamom pods, crushed
  • Fresh ginger, grated (lots)
  • 1 spoonful chai/tea masala
  • A few sprinkles of nutmeg
  • 2–3 spoonfuls loose black tea/chai
  • 2 heaping tbsp brown sugar
  • Parle-G biscuits for dipping

Steps

  1. Add the water to a pot, then the crushed cardamom, chai masala, nutmeg and fresh ginger. Simmer together for a few minutes.
  2. Add the tea, then the milk. Heat until the chai starts rising toward a boil — watch it closely, it moves fast.
  3. Just before boiling, add the brown sugar and stir gently.
  4. Strain into two cups and serve hot with Parle-G.

Moelleux au Chocolat (Melt in the Middle Chocolate Cake)

https://desertislanddishes.co/moelleux-au-chocolat/

A giant melt-in-the-middle chocolate cake — rich, gooey and decadent, especially served warm. Naturally gluten free, just 5 ingredients.

For the cake

  • 1 cup unsalted butter (2 sticks), cut into cubes, plus extra for greasing
  • 8 oz good-quality 70% dark chocolate, roughly chopped (about 1 cup)
  • 2.6 oz cocoa powder (about 3/4 cup)
  • Pinch of salt
  • 7 large eggs
  • 9.3 oz superfine (caster) sugar (about 1 1/3 cups), divided

To serve

  • Heavy (double) cream, or ice cream, or custard

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C/180°C fan). Generously butter a 9-inch (23 cm) round springform pan and line the base with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the butter and chocolate together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water (or microwave in 30-second bursts), stirring until smooth.
  3. Sift in the cocoa powder and salt; stir until smooth. Set aside.
  4. Separate the eggs. Add about 4 3/4 oz (135 g) of the sugar to the yolks and whisk until pale, light and thick.
  5. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks, gradually adding the remaining sugar, until stiff and glossy.
  6. Pour the melted chocolate mixture into the egg yolks and stir gently. Stir in 2 spoonfuls of whisked egg whites vigorously (the "sacrificial" spoon), then gently fold in the remaining whites.
  7. Pour 2/3 of the batter into the prepared pan and bake about 25 minutes, until well risen and a skewer comes out clean. Leave to cool completely in the pan (it may collapse — that is normal).
  8. Keep the remaining 1/3 of batter in the fridge.
  9. Once the cake is cool, remove from the pan, place on a lined baking sheet and spread the reserved batter on top.
  10. Bake again 10–15 minutes, until a thin crust forms but the middle is still soft and jiggly. Rest 10 minutes.
  11. Slice and serve warm with cream, ice cream or custard (or chilled from the fridge).

Marbled Chocolate Chip Cookies

https://handletheheat.com/marbled-chocolate-chip-cookies/

Classic brown sugar chocolate chip cookies and double chocolate chip cookies swirled together.

For the base

  • 2 sticks (227 g) unsalted butter, at cool room temperature
  • 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups (247 g) lightly packed light brown sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla
  • 2 large eggs, at room temperature

For the chocolate chip dough

  • 1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • 3/4 cup (128 g) semisweet chocolate wafers or feves

For the cocoa dough

  • 1 1/4 cups plus 2 tablespoons (175 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup (21 g) unsweetened natural cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon instant espresso powder, optional
  • 3/4 cup (128 g) semisweet chocolate wafers or feves
  • Flaky sea salt, for finishing, if desired

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Beat the butter, granulated sugar and brown sugar until creamy, about 3 minutes. Add vanilla and eggs; beat until combined.
  3. Whisk the chocolate chip dough dry ingredients; add half the creamed butter-sugar mixture (about 330 g) and stir into a cohesive dough. Mix in the chocolate wafers, reserving some for garnish.
  4. To the remaining butter-sugar mixture add the cocoa dough dry ingredients; beat on low to combine. Mix in the chocolate wafers, reserving some for garnish.
  5. Scoop portions of both doughs with a 1 1/2-tablespoon scoop. Tear one ball in half and sandwich the other dough ball inside (or vice versa); roll between palms. Place on the baking sheet and dot with reserved wafers.
  6. If time permits, refrigerate the shaped dough 24–72 hours for thicker, chewier, more flavorful cookies.
  7. Bake 11–13 minutes, until golden at the edges. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt if desired. Cool 5 minutes, then transfer to wire racks.

One-Dish Butter Chicken Pie

Transcribed from reel caption

510 Cal | 53g protein | 30g carbs | 19g fat per pie. Serves 4, made in 2 1/2-cup (600 ml) glass containers.

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 lb (780 g) chicken breast, diced into small pieces
  • 7 oz (200 g) puff pastry (1 serving per pie)

Butter Chicken Base Sauce

  • 1 1/2 cups protein boosted milk (Vitasoy)
  • 3/4 cup (250 g) Greek yogurt
  • 6 tbsp (170 g) tomato paste

Butter Chicken Sauce Spices

  • Use your favorite packet of butter chicken paste, or the homemade mix: 2 tbsp (12 g) curry powder, 1 tbsp (6 g) garam masala, 1 tbsp (6 g) ground cumin, 2 tsp ground ginger, 2 tsp garlic powder, 1.5 tsp salt, 1 tsp turmeric

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the butter chicken base sauce and spices.
  3. Add diced raw chicken to oven-safe containers; divide the milk mixture evenly among them.
  4. Top each container with a square of puff pastry; cut a small slit in the top for steam. Brush pastry with beaten egg.
  5. Place containers on a baking sheet and bake 25–30 minutes, until the pastry is golden brown and the filling is bubbling (chicken internal temp 165°F/74°C).
  6. Let stand 5 minutes before serving. Caution: containers will be hot.

Makhni Chicken (Pakistani-Style Butter Chicken)

https://rookiewithacookie.com/makhni-chicken-its-not-butter-chicken/

Creamy, delicately spiced makhni with no sweet undertones and no onions. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup oil
  • 1 lb chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoon ginger garlic paste
  • 3/4 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili flakes
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, ground
  • 1/4 cup yogurt, lightly beaten
  • 3 tomatoes, pureed (from a can of whole peeled tomatoes)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)

Steps

  1. Fry the chicken in the oil; once the color changes, add salt and ginger garlic paste.
  2. Add the spices and fry 3–4 minutes to coat the chicken; add the yogurt and mix for another two minutes.
  3. Puree the tomatoes and pour into the pan. Bring to a boil, then cook covered on medium heat for 15 minutes.
  4. Remove the lid and cook on high heat ("bhoon") until the water evaporates and the oil separates.
  5. Lower to medium-low; add the heavy cream, black pepper and a few slit green chillies. Cover and simmer 2–3 minutes.
  6. Finish with butter and kasuri methi. Serve hot with naan.

Palakoora Ullikaram (Spinach–Onion Chutney)

Transcribed from reel caption

Ingredients

  • 4 bunches spinach
  • 3 medium onions
  • 1 tbsp oil (first tempering)
  • 1 tsp chana dal
  • 2 tsp coriander seeds
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 5–6 red chillies
  • Salt
  • Small piece of tamarind
  • 2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 2 tbsp oil (second tempering)
  • 1 tsp each cumin seeds, mustard seeds, chana dal
  • 1 tsp garlic

Steps

  1. In a pan with 1 tbsp oil, fry the chana dal, coriander seeds, cumin and red chillies for 5 minutes; transfer to a mixer jar.
  2. In the same oil, fry the onions till golden brown; add salt and a small piece of tamarind. Turn off the flame and cool.
  3. Grind the fried spices to a coarse powder; add the onions and grind again with salt to taste and 2 tsp red chilli powder, coarsely.
  4. In another pan with 2 tbsp oil, add cumin seeds, mustard seeds, chana dal (1 tsp each) and garlic; temper.
  5. Add the spinach (and the ground mixture per the video) and cook through.

  • Curd

  • Roasted cumin powder
  • Garam masala
  • Turmeric
  • Salt
  • Kasuri methi

  • Water

Steps

  1. Cook the onion, ginger, garlic, cashews, green chillies, salt and water; cool completely, add coriander leaves and blend.
  2. Toss the sliced boiled eggs with red chilli powder, salt and turmeric; roast in oil a few seconds and set aside.
  3. Heat oil; add cinnamon, cardamom and cloves, then the blended gravy.
  4. Add curd, black pepper powder, roasted cumin powder, garam masala, turmeric and salt. Cover and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Add kasuri methi and cream, plus water as needed; cover and cook.
  6. Add the eggs, garnish and serve.

Restaurant-Worthy Guacamole

https://playswellwithbutter.com/best-guacamole-recipe-ever/

Chunky-creamy guac with a secret ingredient: Tajín. Ready in 15 minutes; serves 6.

Ingredients

  • 2 large ripe Hass avocados, halved and de-seeded
  • 1/4 medium red onion, finely diced (approx. 1/4 cup)
  • 2 jalapeño peppers, de-seeded as desired and finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
  • 1 large juicy lime, juiced (approx. 3 tbsp)
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped cilantro
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • Heaping 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Heaping 1/2 tsp chile lime seasoning (such as Tajín)
  • For serving: finely chopped cilantro, tortilla chips, etc.

Steps

  1. Smash the aromatics: add the onion, jalapeño and garlic to a resealable bag with a pinch of salt. Seal, pressing out the air, and smash with a rolling pin or heavy pan until juicy and softened but not a paste. (Or use a molcajete.)
  2. Mix: scoop the avocado flesh into a large bowl; add the smashed vegetables and all remaining ingredients (lime juice through Tajín). Smash gently with a spoon to your desired consistency and stir to combine. Taste and adjust salt/lime.
  3. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, topped with extra cilantro and Tajín.
