Chewy Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

https://dumpedlingss.substack.com/p/science-based-chewy-brown-butter

Science-tested chewy brown butter chocolate chip cookies: crisp caramelised edges, a soft bendy centre, deep toffee flavour from browned butter and an overnight rest.

Ingredients (partial — full card on the linked Substack)

  • 225g unsalted butter
  • 30g ice cubes
  • 200g light brown soft sugar
  • 70g caster sugar
  • 40g golden syrup
  • 1 large egg (75–80g)
  • Strong bread flour (swapped for a third of the plain flour)
  • Dark chocolate bars, chopped (not chips)

Steps

  1. Brown the butter until it smells nutty, then add the ice cubes back in so there is enough liquid to properly dissolve the sugars.
  2. Whisk the sugars into the butter in rounds with little rests in between, taking the time to dissolve them fully.
  3. Mix in the egg, golden syrup and flours (with a third of the flour as strong bread flour for chew), then fold through the chopped dark chocolate.
  4. Rest the dough in the fridge for a full 24 hours — it bakes thicker, darker and chewier, and the flavour deepens.
  5. Roll into tall 60g balls (weigh them: every extra 10g adds nearly two minutes of bake time) and bake straight from the fridge.
  6. Pull the cookies while the middles still look underdone — 82°C internal on a probe thermometer gives a soft but not gooey centre.

Vanilla Whip Ganache

Transcribed from reel caption

A stable, pipeable white-chocolate vanilla ganache for cakes and desserts — made ahead and whipped to order.

Ingredients

  • 1 L heavy cream — Cream 1
  • 900 g white chocolate
  • 6 gelatin sheets
  • Vanilla paste — as needed
  • 1 L heavy cream — Cream 2 (cold)

Steps

  1. Bloom the gelatin: soak the sheets in cold water until soft.
  2. Add Cream 1 and the vanilla paste to a saucepan and heat until the cream is just about to boil.
  3. Pour the hot cream over the white chocolate. Add the squeezed-out gelatin and mix well until everything is melted and combined.
  4. Blend until completely smooth and emulsified.
  5. Add the cold Cream 2 and blend again until well combined.
  6. Pour into a clean container, cover and refrigerate for a minimum of 8 hours — overnight is best for a stable whip.
  7. The next day, whip only the amount you need until you reach the desired consistency.

Always keep the ganache cold before whipping for better volume and a more stable texture. Keep the remainder refrigerated and use as needed.

Classic Chocolate Mousse

Transcribed from reel caption (method abridged; full method in video)

A classic French-style chocolate mousse with separated eggs — rich, airy and simple. (Original caption in Portuguese.)

Ingredients

  • 400 g dark chocolate (meio amargo)
  • 100 g unsalted butter
  • 8 eggs, separated into whites and yolks
  • 100 g sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 generous pinch of salt
  • Optional: a splash of cognac or coffee liqueur

Steps

  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter until smooth; let cool slightly.
  2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar until pale and slightly thickened, then blend into the chocolate with the vanilla and salt (plus cognac or coffee liqueur if using).
  3. Whip the egg whites to firm peaks.
  4. Fold the whites into the chocolate in two or three additions, keeping as much air as possible.
  5. Divide into cups and chill until set.

Birthday Cake Fillings: Mousse & Nata Cream

Transcribed from reel caption

Two Brazilian-style cake fillings from an 18th-birthday cake: a brigadeiro-whipped-cream mousse and a nata cream with powdered milk. (Original caption in Portuguese.)

Mousse Filling

  • 3 cans sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 boxes heavy cream (creme de leite)
  • 2 cups whipping cream, whipped firm (chantilly)

Nata Filling

  • 300 g nata (clotted-style cream), very cold
  • 2 cups powdered milk
  • ½ box cold sweetened condensed milk

Steps

  1. Mousse filling: cook the 3 cans of condensed milk with the 2 boxes of cream over heat to make a brigadeiro; set aside.
  2. Whip the 2 cups of whipping cream until very firm, then beat in the brigadeiro until smooth and velvety; reserve.

  4. Add the powdered milk and the condensed milk and keep beating at medium speed until the filling firms up. Ready to use.

Slow Cooker Honey Sesame Chicken

https://theeverydaycookbook.com/slow-cooker-honey-sesame-chicken/

Crockpot-season dinner: chicken cooks low and slow in a sweet-savoury soy, honey and gochujang sauce, then gets shredded with broccoli and tossed with ramen noodles.

Chicken & Sauce

  • 1.5–2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast or thighs
  • ½ cup low sodium soy sauce
  • ½ cup honey
  • 1 tablespoon gochujang
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • ¼ cup ketchup
  • 1 shallot, finely diced
  • ½ yellow onion, diced
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon ginger paste
  • 1–2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • ½ teaspoon crushed red pepper
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Finish & Serve

  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch + 2 tablespoons cold water (slurry)
  • Diced broccoli (fresh or frozen)
  • 2–3 packs ramen noodles
  • Sesame seeds and sliced green onion, for garnish

Steps

  1. Add the chicken to the bottom of the slow cooker.
  2. Add the diced onion, shallot and minced garlic.
  3. Add the soy sauce, honey, gochujang, ketchup, sesame oil and all the seasonings; stir to combine.
  4. Cover and cook on low for 3–4 hours or on high for 2–3 hours.
  5. Remove the chicken and shred it with two forks.
  6. Stir the cornstarch slurry into the sauce in the slow cooker until smooth.
  7. Return the shredded chicken and add the broccoli; cook 30 minutes more (up to 1 hour for frozen broccoli).
  8. Boil the ramen noodles according to the package directions and drain.
  9. Add the noodles to the slow cooker, switch to warm, and toss until every noodle is evenly coated.
  10. Top with sesame seeds and green onions and serve immediately.

Chicken thighs give a juicier result and are more forgiving if they cook a little long. Add the noodles right before serving so they don't go soft. Store the chicken/sauce and any leftover noodles separately.

Mangalorean Prawns Ghee Roast with Neer Dosa

Transcribed from reel caption

Spicy, tangy prawns coated in a glossy ghee-roast masala, served with soft, lacy neer dosa to soak it all up — a little taste of coastal Karnataka.

Marinated Prawns

  • 500g cleaned prawns
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Ghee Roast Masala

  • 10–12 Byadgi chillies, soaked in hot water for 15 mins
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp coriander seeds
  • ½ tsp fenugreek
  • ½ tsp saunf/fennel
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • 5–6 garlic cloves
  • Small piece ginger
  • 1 inch tamarind
  • 1 tsp jaggery (optional)
  • 6–8 cashews, soaked in water for 15–20 mins
  • Salt

Neer Dosa

  • 2 cups raw rice
  • Water
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Marinate the prawns with turmeric, salt and lemon juice for 15–20 minutes.
  2. Dry roast the spices, then grind them with the soaked chillies, garlic, ginger, tamarind, cashews, jaggery and salt into a smooth paste.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp ghee and cook the prawns until just done; remove from the pan.
  4. Add 2–3 tbsp more ghee to the same pan, add the masala and cook on low-medium until glossy and aromatic.
  5. Add the prawns back and toss for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Top with a handful of curry leaves and finish with a generous drizzle of ghee.
  7. For the neer dosa: soak the rice for 4–5 hours, grind into a very smooth batter and add enough water to make it very thin, almost like buttermilk, plus salt.
  8. Pour onto a hot, lightly greased tawa, swirl, cover and cook until set — no flipping needed. Serve hot.

No-Bake French Silk Pudding

https://www.cake-babe.com/no-bake-french-silk-pudding/

French silk pie flavour without the work: silky chocolate pudding layered with Oreo Thins that soften overnight into cake-like layers, topped with whipped cream. No oven required.

Chocolate Pudding

  • 1 box (3.4 oz) instant chocolate pudding mix
  • 1¼ cups cold milk
  • 1 can (13.4 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 packages Oreo Thins (reserve 4–5, crushed, for topping)
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Topping

  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 1 tablespoon powdered sugar

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the cold milk and sweetened condensed milk. Sprinkle the pudding mix over the top and whisk by hand for 2–3 minutes until smooth. Chill 10 minutes to thicken.
  2. While the pudding sets, beat the 2 cups heavy cream with the vanilla until stiff peaks form.
  3. With a rubber spatula, gently fold the whipped cream into the pudding until no streaks remain.
  4. Spread a thin layer of pudding in your dish, cups or trifle bowl, then add a neat layer of whole Oreo Thins. Repeat for 2–3 layers.
  5. Finish with a final layer of pudding that fully covers everything, sealing the edges so the cookies stay soft.
  6. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 6 hours and up to 24 hours.
  7. Right before serving, beat the topping cream with the powdered sugar to medium peaks, spread over the pudding and sprinkle with the reserved crushed Oreos.

Use instant pudding mix, not cook-and-serve. Serves 12; best within 24 hours of assembling.

One-Pan, Speedy Thai Peanut Chickpea Curry (Curry in a Hurry)

https://boldbeanco.com/blogs/beanspo-recipes/store-cupboard-thai-chickpea-curry

Zena Kamgaing's satay-ish chickpea curry from her 'Dinner Time' cookbook, shared on the Bold Bean Co. website — a 15-minute, one-pan Thai peanut curry built on store-cupboard ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 jar Queen Chickpeas — with their bean stock
  • 2 x 400g tins of good-quality coconut milk (70% or more coconut extract)
  • 100g smooth natural peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp good-quality Thai red curry paste
  • 1 tbsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 200ml boiling water
  • 200g baby spinach
  • ½ lime, juice
  • To serve: cooked rice and/or warmed flatbreads; roasted peanuts, finely chopped (optional)

Steps

  1. Place the chickpeas, coconut milk, peanut butter, curry paste, curry powder, soy sauce and sugar in a medium saucepan and set it over a high heat.
  2. Stir in the boiling water and bring the liquid to a simmer, stirring frequently to prevent the curry sauce sticking. Reduce the heat to medium-high and cook, again stirring frequently, for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the baby spinach and lime juice, stirring until the spinach has wilted, then season with salt and pepper.
  4. Serve the curry in bowls with rice and/or flatbreads, and if you want some crunch, scatter over a few roasted peanuts.

Feeds 2–3.

Jhol Maggi (Kathmandu-Inspired)

Nepali-style jhol Maggi — instant noodles in a spicy, brothy, slurpy soup with mustard oil, tangy tomatoes, green chillies and citrusy Timur. Comfort food from the hills.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet Maggi + tastemaker
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil
  • 4–5 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 green chilli, chopped/slit
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp crushed Timur (Nepali Sichuan pepper)
  • Salt, as required
  • 1 3/4–2 cups water
  • Fresh coriander
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Chilli oil/crisp, optional

Steps

  1. Heat the mustard oil until lightly smoking. Add the garlic, onion and green chilli and sauté until aromatic.
  2. Add the tomatoes, chilli powder, turmeric, Timur and a little salt. Cook until the tomatoes completely soften and turn jammy.
  3. Add 1 3/4–2 cups water and simmer for 5–7 minutes to build a flavourful, tangy broth.
  4. Add the Maggi tastemaker and noodles and cook until the noodles are done, keeping plenty of jhol/broth in the pan.
  5. Finish with fresh coriander, a squeeze of lemon and some chilli crisp on top if you like it extra spicy.

Transcribed from the creator's own comment on the reel. No Timur? Use freshly crushed black pepper + a little lemon zest for that peppery, citrusy kick. Serve piping hot.

                                                                            Marbled Chocolate Chip Cookies

                                                                            https://handletheheat.com/marbled-chocolate-chip-cookies/

                                                                            Classic brown sugar chocolate chip cookies and double chocolate chip cookies swirled together.

                                                                            For the base

                                                                            • 2 sticks (227 g) unsalted butter, at cool room temperature
                                                                            • 1/2 cup (100 g) granulated sugar
                                                                            • 1 1/4 cups (247 g) lightly packed light brown sugar
                                                                            • 2 teaspoons vanilla
                                                                            • 2 large eggs, at room temperature

                                                                            For the chocolate chip dough

                                                                            • 1 1/2 cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
                                                                            • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
                                                                            • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
                                                                            • 3/4 cup (128 g) semisweet chocolate wafers or feves

                                                                            For the cocoa dough

                                                                            • 1 1/4 cups plus 2 tablespoons (175 g) all-purpose flour
                                                                            • 1/4 cup (21 g) unsweetened natural cocoa powder
                                                                            • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
                                                                            • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
                                                                            • 1/4 teaspoon instant espresso powder, optional
                                                                            • 3/4 cup (128 g) semisweet chocolate wafers or feves
                                                                            • Flaky sea salt, for finishing, if desired

                                                                            Steps

                                                                            1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
                                                                            2. Beat the butter, granulated sugar and brown sugar until creamy, about 3 minutes. Add vanilla and eggs; beat until combined.
                                                                            3. Whisk the chocolate chip dough dry ingredients; add half the creamed butter-sugar mixture (about 330 g) and stir into a cohesive dough. Mix in the chocolate wafers, reserving some for garnish.
                                                                            4. To the remaining butter-sugar mixture add the cocoa dough dry ingredients; beat on low to combine. Mix in the chocolate wafers, reserving some for garnish.
                                                                            5. Scoop portions of both doughs with a 1 1/2-tablespoon scoop. Tear one ball in half and sandwich the other dough ball inside (or vice versa); roll between palms. Place on the baking sheet and dot with reserved wafers.
                                                                            6. If time permits, refrigerate the shaped dough 24–72 hours for thicker, chewier, more flavorful cookies.
                                                                            7. Bake 11–13 minutes, until golden at the edges. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt if desired. Cool 5 minutes, then transfer to wire racks.

                                                                            Moelleux au Chocolat (Melt in the Middle Chocolate Cake)

                                                                            https://desertislanddishes.co/moelleux-au-chocolat/

                                                                            A giant melt-in-the-middle chocolate cake — rich, gooey and decadent, especially served warm. Naturally gluten free, just 5 ingredients.

                                                                            For the cake

                                                                            • 1 cup unsalted butter (2 sticks), cut into cubes, plus extra for greasing
                                                                            • 8 oz good-quality 70% dark chocolate, roughly chopped (about 1 cup)
                                                                            • 2.6 oz cocoa powder (about 3/4 cup)
                                                                            • Pinch of salt
                                                                            • 7 large eggs
                                                                            • 9.3 oz superfine (caster) sugar (about 1 1/3 cups), divided

                                                                            To serve

                                                                            • Heavy (double) cream, or ice cream, or custard

                                                                            Steps

                                                                            1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C/180°C fan). Generously butter a 9-inch (23 cm) round springform pan and line the base with parchment paper.
                                                                            2. Melt the butter and chocolate together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water (or microwave in 30-second bursts), stirring until smooth.
                                                                            3. Sift in the cocoa powder and salt; stir until smooth. Set aside.
                                                                            4. Separate the eggs. Add about 4 3/4 oz (135 g) of the sugar to the yolks and whisk until pale, light and thick.
                                                                            5. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks, gradually adding the remaining sugar, until stiff and glossy.
                                                                            6. Pour the melted chocolate mixture into the egg yolks and stir gently. Stir in 2 spoonfuls of whisked egg whites vigorously (the "sacrificial" spoon), then gently fold in the remaining whites.
                                                                            7. Pour 2/3 of the batter into the prepared pan and bake about 25 minutes, until well risen and a skewer comes out clean. Leave to cool completely in the pan (it may collapse — that is normal).
                                                                            8. Keep the remaining 1/3 of batter in the fridge.
                                                                            9. Once the cake is cool, remove from the pan, place on a lined baking sheet and spread the reserved batter on top.
                                                                            10. Bake again 10–15 minutes, until a thin crust forms but the middle is still soft and jiggly. Rest 10 minutes.
                                                                            11. Slice and serve warm with cream, ice cream or custard (or chilled from the fridge).
